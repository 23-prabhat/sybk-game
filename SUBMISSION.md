# SYBK · Show Your Ball Knowledge

**Tagline**

Five memes, one guess each, and a pigeon who knows when you’re bluffing.

## Devpost description

SYBK is a daily internet-culture quiz hosted by Pip, a pigeon emcee. A Reddit Interactive Post opens five shared clues, from silhouettes and captions to audio and scrambled tiles. Take one shot at each answer. Pip’s first hint is free, while trading for a stronger hint changes the question’s value from 100 points to 70.

After the round, share a spoiler-free score, compare daily and weekly standings, or vote on the weekend’s theme. Live Duels give two Reddit players a shared buzzer. The first buzz earns the floor, and a miss gives the other player a chance to steal. Friend rooms work with a share link or a room code. After Dark is separately labeled and requires Reddit’s 18+ content setting and explicit confirmation.

## Judge walkthrough

1. Open the demo post and play **Daily Solo**. Ask Pip for a free hint, then guess or skip to see the reveal.
2. Finish the five clues to see the result. Sign in to Reddit to enter the rankings and vote on a weekend theme.
3. For a duel, open **Live Duels** and create a room. Join from a second Reddit account using the room code. Both players choose **Ready**, then buzz for the floor.
